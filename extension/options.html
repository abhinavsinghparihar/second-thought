04

Privacy & security

Exactly what leaves your browser, and what does not.

Local mode
Nothing leaves your browser. The requirement engine is plain JavaScript that runs in this extension.
AI mode
Only the text of the message you were about to send is sent — to the provider you chose, from your browser, using your key. There is no Second Thought server.
Keys
Kept in chrome.storage.local. Not synced, not logged, not included in error messages.
History
Off by default. When on, refined prompts stay in chrome.storage.local until you clear them.
Your original
Never discarded. If anything fails, the untouched message is sent exactly as you typed it.
