Second Thought is the pause between intention and send. Local. Fail-safe. Never invents a fact.

Why

What it’s forWhat it’s for

You type a normal request in ChatGPT. Important context is often missing. Second Thought is the requirement-discovery layer in front of Send: it notices the gap, asks a few sharp questions, then lets you review a refined prompt in the same chat.

Product

What it doesWhat it does

  1. Intercepts Send and snapshots your original text first.
  2. Decides on-device in ~120ms. No API key required.
  3. Pass-through for greetings and factual questions — “What is the capital of India?” is never interrupted.
  4. Clarify: 5–6 MCQs immediately, free text in any language.
  5. You review. Nothing invented. Insert + submit in the same composer.
  6. Fail-safe: original still sends. History default off. Keys stay local.
